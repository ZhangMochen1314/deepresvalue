# Account Settings

In account settings, you can manage personal information and view your invite code.

## Accessing Settings

Click the **avatar area** at the bottom of the sidebar to enter the user settings page.

## Configurable Items

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Nickname** | Set your display name |
| **Avatar** | Upload a custom profile picture |
| **Phone Number** | Change your bound phone number |

## Credit Balance

- The top-right corner of the top bar displays your current credit balance in real time
- Click the credit display area to view detailed consumption history

## Theme Switching

The platform offers five interface themes. Click the theme button on the left edge of the sidebar to switch:

| Theme | Style | Best For |
|-------|-------|----------|
| Follow System | Auto-matches OS theme | General, automatic |
| Light | Neutral blue-white, clear and bright | Everyday use |
| Dark | Cool gray-blue dark background | Nighttime use |
| Solarized | Classic muted color scheme | Extended reading |
| Warm | Beige warm tone, soft contrast | Extended reading, eye comfort |

> Theme switching takes effect immediately — no page refresh required.
