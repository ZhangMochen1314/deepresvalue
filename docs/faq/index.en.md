# Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using DeepResValue, organized by category.

## Registration & Login

### How do I register?

Visit [deepresvalue.top](https://deepresvalue.top), click "Get Access" or "Login" in the top-right corner. Register with your phone number and SMS verification code. New users automatically receive starter credits.

### Are conversation records saved permanently?

Yes. All conversations are stored in the cloud, linked to your account. Access them from any device after logging in. To delete, right-click a conversation in the sidebar and select "Delete" (irreversible).

## Feature Usage

### I can't code at all. Can I really use this?

Absolutely. DeepResValue is designed for "zero code." Just describe your analysis needs in Chinese — the AI handles everything from method selection to code writing to result output. The platform's core users are undergraduate students with no programming background.

### Are AI-generated results reliable? Can they be used in defense?

Yes. DeepResValue uses frontier LLMs, validated through thousands of real research projects. Most importantly, every analysis step preserves complete code and execution logs — when committee members challenge your methods, display the evidence chain directly. 100% reproducible.

### What if I don't have data?

The platform includes built-in city/provincial/county macro panels and listed company financial panels. Data collection skills also access financial markets, health, medical, biological, and literature external data sources. Describe your needs in Chinese and the AI returns structured data — no middlemen, no cost.

### What file formats are supported?

80+ formats across 11 categories: statistics/data (.csv, .xlsx, .dta, .sav, .parquet, etc.), documents (.pdf, .docx, .txt), images (.png, .jpg), archives (.zip, .rar), scripts (.py, .R, .do, .m), spatial data (.shp, .geojson), bioinformatics (.fasta, .vcf), and more. Max single file: **100MB**, max **10 files** per conversation.

### Is there a file size limit?

Max 100MB per file, max 10 files per conversation. For oversized files, consider splitting or compressing first.

### How many sessions can run simultaneously?

Maximum **2 concurrent sessions** per user. When 2 tasks are running, new submissions prompt a wait message. Wait for any task to finish, or right-click a running conversation in the sidebar to stop it and free a slot.

## AIGC Related

### AI traces are too obvious — what about AIGC detection?

DeepResValue includes a built-in AIGC detection reduction engine: 21 AI hot-word auto-replacement + Best-of-N rewriting + scene-aware humanization. Testing shows AIGC detection rates reduced from 85% to below the safe threshold.

## Usage Issues

### AI response was cut off — what do I do?

1. Refresh the page and reopen the conversation — in most cases, history is preserved.
2. If content was truly truncated, send "please continue" in the input box — the AI resumes from the breakpoint.
3. If problems persist, contact support via the "Help" entry at the bottom of the sidebar.

### Does the platform support team collaboration?

The current version does not support real-time multi-user collaboration. To share analysis results, export AI-generated content as PDF or Markdown and share with teammates.
