# Mentor Mode

Mentor Mode is your 24/7 academic advisor — no topic direction? Unsure about methodology? Not sure if your research idea is feasible? The AI can guide you step by step through research design.

## Who It's For

- Master's and doctoral students
- Research beginners and undergraduate thesis writers
- Researchers needing methodology guidance
- Students at the topic selection stage who are uncertain about research feasibility

## Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Guided Dialogue | AI proactively asks about your research direction and needs, progressively narrowing scope |
| Methodology Recommendation | Recommends the most suitable research methods and analytical frameworks based on your data and questions |
| Feasibility Assessment | Evaluates academic value, data availability, and methodological fit of your topic |
| Step-by-Step Guidance | Breaks complex research tasks into executable steps |
| Instant Response | Available for discussion even late at night — 100× faster than waiting for your advisor's WeChat reply |

## Example Prompts

> "Is this topic feasible?"

> "Which is better for my data, DID or DML?"

> "I want to study the impact of digital transformation on corporate innovation — help me design the research framework."

## How to Switch

1. **Enter the conversation interface** — Find the mode selection entry in the input box area at the bottom of the main interface.
2. **Select Mentor Mode** — Click the mode toggle button and choose "Mentor Mode" from the options.
3. **Start the conversation** — The switch takes effect immediately. Enter your research-related questions to begin.

> Switching modes does not affect existing conversation history, but starting a new conversation with the new mode is recommended for the best experience.
