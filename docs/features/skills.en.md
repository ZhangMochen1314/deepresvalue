DeepResValue includes **100+ professional skills** covering the full research workflow. Skills have been **validated through thousands of real research projects** and are at the **global cutting edge**. Selecting a matching skill causes the AI to load domain-specific professional knowledge and analysis tools, significantly improving output quality. All research data is built-in — just state your needs and the Agent calls autonomously. All frontier research analysis methods are supported — no method is out of reach; the Agent writes code in real time guided by specialized skills and executes in the cloud.

## How Skills Work

Selecting a skill is equivalent to loading a **domain-specific knowledge base** for the AI:

- **Empirical Analysis skills**: The AI understands the applicable conditions of various econometric methods and hypothesis testing procedures
- **Competition skills**: The AI is familiar with competition scoring criteria and award-winning paper writing patterns
- **Discipline-specific skills**: The AI loads the corresponding discipline's professional tool library and terminology system

## Usage Steps

1. **Open the skill panel** — Find the skill selection entry in the conversation interface.
2. **Select the appropriate skill** — Browse and choose the most matching skill based on your research needs.
3. **Start the conversation** — After selecting a skill, enter your question normally. The AI provides more precise output based on the selected skill's knowledge base.
