# Research Experts

DeepResValue includes **129 Research Experts** covering the full research workflow. Each expert is a packaged combination of domain-specific professional workflows and tools. Selecting a matching expert causes the AI to load domain-specific professional knowledge and analysis tools, significantly improving output quality. All research data is built-in — just state your needs and the Agent calls autonomously. All frontier research analysis methods are supported — no method is out of reach; the Agent writes code in real time guided by specialized experts and executes in the cloud.

## How Experts Work

Selecting an expert is equivalent to loading a **domain-specific knowledge base** for the AI:

- **Empirical Analysis experts**: The AI understands the applicable conditions of various econometric methods and hypothesis testing procedures
- **Competition experts**: The AI is familiar with competition scoring criteria and award-winning paper writing patterns
- **Discipline-specific experts**: The AI loads the corresponding discipline's professional tool library and terminology system

## Usage Steps

1. **Open the expert panel** — Find the expert selection entry in the conversation interface.
2. **Select the appropriate expert** — Browse and choose the most matching expert based on your research needs.
3. **Start the conversation** — After selecting an expert, enter your question normally. The AI provides more precise output based on the selected expert's knowledge base.

## Example Scenarios

> After selecting the "Full-Workflow Empirical Analysis" expert:
