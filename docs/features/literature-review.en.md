# Literature Review

AI automatically searches Chinese and English literature, extracts key findings, and generates structured review reports.

## Features

- Automatically searches the latest research in relevant fields (NCPSSD Chinese journals, CNKI enhanced abstracts, arXiv English preprints, PubMed biomedical — four-source search)
- Classifies and organizes by topic, method, and conclusion
- Automatically cites source references
- Generates reference lists conforming to academic standards

## Step-by-Step Usage

1. **Describe the review topic** — State your research field, focus areas, and time range in the chat.
2. **AI conducts online search** — The AI automatically searches Chinese and English academic databases and screens relevant literature.
3. **Receive structured review** — The AI classifies literature by topic, method, and conclusion, producing a structured review report with all sources cited.

## Example Prompts

> "Write a literature review on digital economy and carbon emissions, citing recent 3-year core journals."

> "Search for the latest research on the relationship between corporate ESG performance and financial performance — 10 Chinese and 10 English papers, classified by conclusion."

> "Help me outline research progress on machine learning in medical image diagnosis, focusing on breakthroughs after 2022."

## Citation Traceability

Literature cited in AI responses is marked with superscript markers (e.g., `[1]`, `[2]`). Click to view full source details, ensuring academic citation accuracy and traceability.

> All citations are clickable for original source verification, making it easy to verify and cite.
