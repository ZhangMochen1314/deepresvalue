# Competition Modeling

DeepResValue includes **129 Research Experts** covering all academic disciplines. Competition-specific modules are continuously updated to ensure every national-level competition has dedicated expert guidance.

## Supported Competitions

### 🔢 Mathematical Modeling (CUMCM / MCM-ICM)

- Covers **CUMCM (China Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling)** and **MCM/ICM (USA Mathematical Contest in Modeling / Interdisciplinary Contest in Modeling)**
- AI-assisted modeling + automated programming + LaTeX typesetting output
- Differential equations, optimization models, neural networks, stochastic processes, spatial econometrics — full coverage
- Full workflow from problem analysis to 20-page paper

### 🏆 National Statistical Modeling Competition

- Dedicated `DeepResValue-StatModel` expert
- Covers the full workflow: topic selection → data processing → model construction → report writing
- DML / DID / RDD / Causal Forest / Synthetic Control — **all methods commonly used in award-winning submissions are fully supported**

### 🚀 Challenge Cup

- Covers **"Challenge Cup" National Undergraduate Extracurricular Academic Science & Technology Works Competition** and **Entrepreneurship Plan Competition**
- Built-in dedicated experts for topic selection, research design, data analysis, and report writing
- AI-assisted market analysis + financial forecasting + risk assessment

### 💡 Innovation & Entrepreneurship (三创赛)

- Covers **National E-Commerce "Innovation, Creativity & Entrepreneurship" Challenge**
- Full workflow expert: innovation project initiation → business planning → practice verification → pitch defense
- Business plans, market research analysis, data visualization, pitch decks — one-stop output

## Step-by-Step Usage

1. **Select the competition expert** — In the expert panel, choose the dedicated competition expert (e.g., StatModel, Mathematical Modeling).
2. **Describe the competition problem** — Paste the competition problem or requirements into the chat, explain your approach and where you need help.
3. **Complete tasks step by step** — The AI guides and produces output following the competition workflow (topic selection → data processing → model construction → report writing).
