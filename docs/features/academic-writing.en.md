# Academic Writing

DeepResValue covers every stage of paper writing — from topic selection to defense, full-cycle support.

## Covered Writing Stages

| Stage | What AI Helps With |
|-------|-------------------|
| **Topic Selection** | Evaluate topic feasibility, recommend frontier directions |
| **Proposal** | Auto-generate proposal framework, populate literature review |
| **Body Writing** | Polish paragraphs, optimize expression, check academic standards |
| **Data Analysis** | 100+ methods auto-matched, three-line tables + charts |
| **Format Check** | Auto-check reference formatting and layout standards |
| **Defense Prep** | Generate defense PPT outline, organize defense logic |

## Step-by-Step Usage

1. **Identify your writing stage** — Tell the AI which stage you are in (topic selection / proposal / body / format check / defense prep).
2. **Provide background information** — Describe your research topic, discipline, existing materials, and other context.
3. **Receive writing output** — The AI generates corresponding academic content based on your stage and needs.

## Example Prompts

> "Evaluate the feasibility of the topic 'The impact of digital inclusive finance on rural entrepreneurship' — analyze data availability and method choice."

> "Generate a proposal framework for my research on 'The impact of carbon emission trading on corporate green innovation'."

> "Academic-polish the following paragraph while maintaining scholarly style and improving logical coherence."

> "Help me organize my defense logic. My thesis is titled 'Research on the employment polarization effect of AI on the labor market'."

> In Academic Mode, AI output automatically conforms to academic standards including citation format, paragraph structure, and expression style.

> AI-generated academic content should be personalized before use to ensure it reflects your research characteristics and advisor's requirements.
