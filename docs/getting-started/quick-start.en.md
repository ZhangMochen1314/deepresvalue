# Quick Start

Get started with DeepResValue in just a few minutes.

## Register & Login

1. **Visit the platform** — Open your browser and go to [deepresvalue.top](https://deepresvalue.top).
2. **Create an account** — Click **"Get Access"** or **"Login"** in the top-right corner. Register with your phone number and SMS verification code.
3. **Enter the main interface** — After registration you are automatically redirected to the main workspace.

> New users receive complimentary starter credits upon registration, granting immediate access to all features.

## Create Your First Conversation

1. **Start a new conversation** — Click the **"+ New Conversation"** button at the top of the sidebar.
2. **Enter your question** — Type your question or task description in the input box at the bottom, then press **Enter**.
3. **View results** — The AI begins analysis automatically and returns results in a streaming fashion.

## Writing Effective Prompts

The more specific your prompt, the more precise the AI output.

| Quality | Example |
|---------|---------|
| ❌ Vague | "Help me do an analysis" |
| ✅ Specific | "Use DID to analyze the impact of digital transformation on corporate innovation, controlling for industry and year fixed effects, run parallel trend and placebo tests, output three-line tables" |

> Tip: Stating your full requirements in one message saves credits by avoiding repeated back-and-forth.

## Three Usage Modes

DeepResValue provides three modes you can switch between at any time:

### 🎓 Mentor Mode

Your 24/7 academic advisor. Lost on topic selection? Unsure about methodology? The AI guides you step by step through research design, recommends optimal methods, and assesses feasibility.

> Try asking: "Is this topic feasible?" / "Which is better for my data, DID or DML?"

### 📝 Academic Mode

Full-service paper workflow — from proposal to graduation thesis, from literature review to defense slides, all in one place. Built-in AIGC detection reduction engine ensures confident submission.

> Try asking: "Write a literature review on digital economy and carbon emissions, citing recent 3-year core journals"

### ⚡ Professional Mode

Stata-grade analysis, no coding required. Describe your analysis needs in Chinese — the AI automatically writes Python/R code, runs regressions, and generates publication-ready tables and charts.

> Try asking: "Use DID to analyze this panel data, run parallel trend tests, output three-line tables"
