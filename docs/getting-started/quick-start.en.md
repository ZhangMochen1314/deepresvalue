# Quick Start

> 🚧 This page is under construction. For the complete English guide, see [user-guide.en.md](../user-guide.en.md).

## Registration & Login

1. Visit [deepresvalue.top](https://deepresvalue.top)
2. Click **"Get Access"** or **"Login"**
3. Register with your phone number and SMS verification code

## Create Your First Conversation

1. Click **"+ New Conversation"** in the sidebar
2. Type your question and press **Enter**
3. AI begins analysis and returns results in streaming fashion
